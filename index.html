About Us

Founded in 2015, Sonii Bakery started as a family bakery in the heart of the city. Today, we're a team of 50+ passionate bakers serving 10,000+ customers annually. We source organic ingredients, support local suppliers, and innovate with flavors like matcha green tea and salted caramel. Our mission: To spread joy through cakes. We've catered events for celebrities, won "Best Dessert Shop" awards, and expanded to online orders with free delivery in major cities.

Fun fact: Our bakers have created cakes as tall as 5 feet for grand celebrations!
